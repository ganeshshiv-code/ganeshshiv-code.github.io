About Me

I solve business problems with data — and I start with the question, not the tool.

I'm Shiv Ganesh, a Data Analyst based in India. What I enjoy most is the moment a messy dataset turns into a clear answer a manager can act on: which customers are about to leave, whether an intervention actually worked, and what to do on Monday.

I work with SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau and Excel across the full analytics lifecycle — data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistics and A/B testing, data modeling, dashboards and production automation. My four PulseMetrics projects follow one SaaS business case from churn diagnosis through predictive modeling, a controlled experiment and a scheduled scoring pipeline — each independently verified.

Before analytics, I spent 3+ years on the front line of fintech, travel, gaming and gig-economy platforms — Google Pay, American Airlines, Roblox, Uber. That taught me how customers actually behave, how processes break, and what churn looks like before it shows up in a table. The methods — cohort analysis, churn and retention modeling, A/B testing, KPI design — are the same whether the business sells software, financial products, retail goods or rides, and I'm open to analyst roles across product, finance, operations and beyond.
